# Code of Conduct — maatify/email-delivery

[![Maatify Ecosystem](https://img.shields.io/badge/Maatify-Ecosystem-9C27B0?style=for-the-badge)](https://github.com/Maatify)

## Our standard

Participants are expected to communicate respectfully, constructively and in good faith.
Technical disagreement is welcome; personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, intimidation
and deliberate disruption are not.

## Scope

This applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and other project spaces
used for contribution or support.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to **support@maatify.dev** with relevant context. Do not include
credentials or unrelated sensitive information. Reports will be reviewed and handled according to
the project's applicable governance, authorization and license arrangements. Security vulnerabilities
follow the separate [security reporting route](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may clarify, moderate or restrict participation when necessary to protect a respectful
and productive project space.
